CRA · Clinical Research Assistant Executive brief

Research is the part that needs a human.
Everything else is mechanics.

CRA runs the full arc of clinical research — evidence synthesis, trial design, regulatory start-up, data collection, statistical analysis, and publication drafting — on one governed platform, under the standards a submission must survive: ICH GCP, 21 CFR Part 11, PRISMA 2020, CDISC. Your researchers put their judgment on the science. The platform does the rest, and proves it did it correctly.

Methodological integrity

This is not “AI-generated research”

The distinction that matters to a reviewer: who did the science, and can every number be traced. CRA is built so the answers are your researchers and yes.

The AI automates the mechanical work and structures the workflow for compliance. It is architecturally prevented from inventing the science.

Citations only from real searches

The platform rejects any answer quoting effect sizes, confidence intervals, or PMIDs from the model’s memory. Every citation traces to an actual literature search executed during the session.

Statistics computed, never estimated

Every pooled effect, survival curve, and forest plot is produced by real statistical code running on real data in an isolated sandbox — the model orchestrates the analysis; it cannot make up the result.

Provenance on every number

Each figure in a study report carries a machine-checked reference to the source artefact it was derived from. GRADE certainty is computed from the domain ratings — never asserted.

Structured, stage-gated workflows

A meta-analysis cannot skip from question to conclusion: the protocol, search, screening, and extraction stages are enforced in order, with the researcher confirming each gate.

Where the time goes

Offload the mechanics, keep the thinking

Most of a study’s calendar time is consumed by work that is essential, skilled — and mechanical. CRA absorbs it.

Hours, not weeks

Boolean search strategies with MeSH expansion, PRISMA-P protocols, screening logs, extraction tables, SAP boilerplate, registry field mapping, consent-form compliance checks — drafted in minutes, reviewed by your team, finalized the same day.

Compliance as a property, not a project

Audit trails, electronic signatures with password re-authentication, delegation logs, separation-of-duties checks, and inspector-ready PDF reports accumulate automatically as the team works — not as a pre-submission scramble.

Nothing falls through

The serious-adverse-event clock, visit windows, query resolution, drug reconciliation, and training expiries are tracked by the system and surfaced before they become findings.

Higher-order work stays human

Framing the question, judging clinical relevance, interpreting heterogeneity, deciding what the evidence means — the work that makes research research gets the researcher’s full attention.

Evidence access & operations

The literature your license already covers

Open + subscription sources

PubMed and Europe PMC are built in. Connect your institution’s publisher credentials — Elsevier titles such as The Lancet, and other subscription sources — with per-source API keys, rate limits, and usage caps managed from the admin console.

Your private evidence library

Upload PDFs and cached publications into a local, searchable library; the AI grounds its answers in what your team has actually collected, with citations back to each passage.

Your cloud, your data

Deploys into your own AWS account — private network, single sign-on, role-based access with separation-of-duties, and PHI kept in a dedicated store. Nothing leaves your environment.

Every deliverable, export-ready

Protocols, SAPs, study reports, GRADE tables, manuscripts, and regulatory logs export as formatted PDF and Word documents — submission-shaped from day one.
